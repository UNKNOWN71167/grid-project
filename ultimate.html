EAFC 26 Ultimate Team Guide

Ultimate Team is one of the most popular modes in EAFC 26. In this mode players build their own dream squad by collecting player cards, completing challenges, and playing matches to earn coins and rewards. You can compete online against other players or play offline modes while improving your team over time.

Building Your Ultimate Team

Choose a Good Starter Squad

When starting Ultimate Team you will receive starter players. Try to build a squad where players come from the same league or nationality so they have better chemistry.

Chemistry

Chemistry is very important in Ultimate Team. Players perform better when they have strong chemistry links with teammates from the same league, club, or country.

Earn Coins

Coins can be earned by playing matches, completing objectives, and finishing Squad Building Challenges. These coins can then be used to buy better players from the transfer market.

Ultimate Team Tips

Squad Building Challenges (SBCs)

SBCs allow players to exchange squads of players for rewards such as packs, coins, or special player cards. Completing SBCs is one of the fastest ways to improve your team.

Play Division Rivals

Division Rivals is an online mode where players compete against others of similar skill level. Winning matches gives weekly rewards which can help upgrade your squad.

Open Packs Wisely

Packs contain random players and items. Saving packs for special events or promotions can increase your chances of getting better players.

Upgrade Your Team Slowly

Instead of buying one expensive player, try to upgrade multiple positions to create a balanced team.

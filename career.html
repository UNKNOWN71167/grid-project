EAFC 26 Career Mode Guide

Career Mode is one of the most popular modes in EAFC 26 where you can manage a football club or play as a single player. You control transfers, tactics, training, youth development, and try to win trophies with your team. This guide will help you understand how to start, what teams to pick, and the best strategies to succeed in Career Mode.

How to Start Career Mode

Choose a Team

When starting Career Mode, the first thing you need to do is choose a club. Beginners should pick strong teams like Real Madrid, Manchester City, or Bayern Munich because they already have strong squads and big budgets.

Set Difficulty

EAFC 26 allows players to choose the difficulty level. Beginners should start on Professional or World Class difficulty and increase it later as they improve.

Choose Manager or Player Career

In Manager Career Mode you control the entire team including tactics, transfers, training and youth academy. In Player Career Mode you control one player and try to become the best footballer in the world.

Career Mode Tips

Youth Academy

One of the best ways to build a successful team is through the Youth Academy. You can scout young talents from different countries and develop them into world-class players.

Smart Transfers

Always look for young players with high potential. Signing players under the age of 23 can help your team grow stronger over multiple seasons.

Training and Development

Training helps players improve their overall rating and develop new skills. Make sure to train important players regularly to maximize their potential.

Use Your Budget Wisely

Do not spend your entire transfer budget on one player. Try to improve multiple positions so your squad becomes balanced.

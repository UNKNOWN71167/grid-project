EAFC 26 Clubs Guide

Clubs mode in EAFC 26 allows players to create a virtual footballer and play online with friends as one team. Each player controls their own character on the field and works together to win matches against other clubs around the world. This mode focuses on teamwork, communication, and skill development.

Getting Started

Create Your Pro

The first step in Clubs mode is creating your virtual player. You can customize their appearance, position, and playstyle. Choosing the right position helps the team perform better.

Join or Create a Club

Players can either create their own club or join a friend's club. Each club competes in divisions and tries to move up to higher leagues by winning matches.

Level Up Your Player

As you play matches, your player earns experience points and levels up. This allows you to unlock new skills, attributes, and playstyles that improve your performance on the field.

Clubs Tips

Teamwork is Important

Unlike other modes, Clubs requires strong teamwork. Passing, positioning, and communication with teammates are key to winning matches.

Choose the Right Position

Playing a position that fits your playstyle will help your team. For example, creative players may enjoy midfield positions, while fast players might prefer winger or striker roles.

Upgrade Your Skills

Use skill points to improve attributes like pace, shooting, dribbling, or defending depending on your role. This makes your player stronger and more effective.

Play Regular Matches

The more matches you play, the faster your player improves. Winning matches also helps your club move up divisions and compete against stronger teams.
